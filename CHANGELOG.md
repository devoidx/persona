# Persona

## v0.7.6
- Bloodthirsty: a lifetime trait for anyone with 100,000+ creatures killed, from the Statistics panel. Creatures killed now shows on the Lifetime tab.

## v0.7.5
- Works with KillDex: if it's installed, Habits gets a Kills section and three new traits: Grudge Match, Rare Hunter and Exterminator.
- Vanity pets are tracked on WoW: Forever too.
- Fixed: Gravity Enthusiast, Wanderer and Stable Master could take over the title on long-played characters. They still appear, but no longer crowd out your other habits.

## v0.7.4
- Combat pets: Old Friend for any class with a pet, plus Stable of Beasts and Lone Wolf for hunters. The Habits tab shows your combat pets and how long each one is out.
- Fixed: time shares like "pet out" counted from before tracking started, and jumped around for the first few minutes. AFK, stealth, animal-form and pet history restart once.

## v0.7.3
- Vanity pets: Never Alone, Best Friends and Menagerie, plus a Vanity pets section on Habits.
- Opened from the character screen, the panel now closes with it, even if moved.
- Places: time in open ground between named places is labelled as such.

## v0.7.2
- EllesmereUI users: Persona now matches your UI's look (window, buttons, fonts and accent colour).
  Turn it off in EllesmereUI under Blizzard Skins+ > Window Skins > Third-Party Addons.
- Who's who now gives each character one line: the trait they're most known for.

## v0.7.1
- Share your profile: the Share button (or /persona share) gives you a copy-ready
  version for Discord or in-game chat.

## v0.7.0
- Class and racial signature moves: Taxi Service, Drama Queen, Hiding From Nobody,
  Never Owned a Horse, Lurker and more, counted outside combat only.
- /persona moves lists every move Persona counts on your character.
- Fixed: druids and shamans in travel form were counted as walking.

## v0.6.1
- New traits: Hoarder, Travels Light, Nemesis (where you keep dying) and Easily Distracted.
- Every trait now has five phrasings, so your profile reads differently from day to day.
- Habits shows bag fullness and AFK time; Places shows where you die.

## v0.6.0
- New Roster tab: every character on your account, who's the most restless,
  the clumsiest and so on, plus account traits like Altoholic and One True Main.
- /persona forget Name removes a character from the roster.

## v0.5.5
- The panel now has tabs: Profile, Habits, Emotes, Lifetime and Places.
- Traits that contradict each other no longer appear together.

## v0.5.4
- Minimap button: click to open, right-click to dock. /persona minimap hides it.
- Persona also appears in the minimap's addons button.

## v0.5.3
- Lifetime traits now work on every client language, not just English

## v0.5.2
- Movable panel

## v0.5.1

  Existing /read counts are cleared.

## v0.5.0
- Persona now notices your emotes: Signature Move, Dancer, Polite, Rude and Dances Alone.
- New trait: Stone-Faced, for long-played characters who have never used /lol.
